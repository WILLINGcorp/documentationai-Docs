<Update label="2024-11-15" description="v1.3.0" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## New Features

- Added automated 2257 signature delegation with a consent flow for eligible creators who want future paperwork handled through the platform.

## Improvements

- Added settings controls so creators can enable or revoke automatic 2257 signing.
- Added a delegation audit view in the vault and compliance areas so teams can review when automated signing was authorized, used, or revoked.
- Updated the verification flow so creators enter birth dates through date pickers and submit ID images with either **Take Photo** or **Upload**.

## Bug Fixes

- Corrected profile verification flow for business accounts.


</Update>
