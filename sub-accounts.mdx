## Manage organization access without shared logins

Sub-accounts let each team member sign in with their own ModelBoard profile while acting on behalf of a Business, Studio, or Enterprise organization.

Every sub-account belongs to an organization, has an owner or staff role, and appears in the account switcher after access is approved. This keeps team actions tied to a person instead of a shared organization password.

<Callout kind="info">

Use sub-accounts for every person who needs organization access. Shared logins make approvals, capability reviews, and audit trails harder to trust.

</Callout>

## Roles and capabilities

ModelBoard uses two sub-account roles: **Owner** and **Staff**. Owners always have full access. Staff access starts with defaults and can be changed from the organization's **Capability Matrix**.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Owner" icon="shield">

Owners manage the organization's identity and team access.

Owners can:

- Approve, reject, and invite sub-accounts
- Review incoming and outgoing transfer requests
- Change staff access in the Capability Matrix
- Reset staff capability overrides to the default set
- Manage sensitive organization settings

Assign owner access only to people who should be trusted with the organization's identity, staff access, and sensitive configuration.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Staff" icon="users">

Staff members handle operational work without receiving full ownership control.

Default staff capabilities include:

- Posting studio news
- Creating casting forms
- Uploading to organization storage
- Managing collaboration projects
- Sending organization messages

Staff do not receive sensitive capabilities by default, including editing the organization profile, managing billing, approving sub-accounts, managing BioLink, or signing compliance as the organization.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### Capability Matrix behavior

The Capability Matrix shows a row for each organization capability and columns for **Owner** and **Staff**. Owners always show as allowed for every capability.

Owners can toggle staff capabilities, then choose **Save** to apply changes. They can also use **Discard** to abandon unsaved changes or **Reset defaults** to restore the built-in staff capability set.

<Callout kind="tip">

Capability changes apply immediately to every staff sub-account in the organization. Review the matrix before granting staff access to approval, billing, compliance, or profile-management actions.

</Callout>

## Sub-Account Requests

Owners manage access requests from the **Sub-Account Requests** card. The card shows a pending count and separates work into four tabs so owners can act on the right type of request.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Invites" icon="mail">

The **Invites** tab contains pending legacy invitations that still need to be sent.

Use **Send** to create the invited sub-account and send the invitee a password setup email. Legacy invitation rows may show a source badge so owners can understand where the pending invitation came from.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Join" icon="user-plus">

The **Join** tab contains new sub-account requests from people who want to join the organization.

Owners can **Approve** a request to grant organization access or **Reject** it to close the request without granting access. Rejections can include an optional reason.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Incoming" icon="arrow-right-left">

The **Incoming** tab contains transfer requests where a sub-account is moving into your organization.

Approve an incoming transfer only after you verify the person, the source organization, and the reason for the move.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Outgoing" icon="arrow-right-left">

The **Outgoing** tab contains transfer requests where a sub-account linked to your organization is moving to another organization.

Use this tab to approve or reject the release side of the transfer. Once the required approvals are complete, the sub-account's organization connection updates to the destination organization.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Invite or approve a sub-account

Use this workflow when you need to add someone to an organization or finish an existing pending invitation.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the organization context" icon="building2">

Switch into the Business, Studio, or Enterprise organization that should own the team member's access.

Confirm you are acting as the right organization before reviewing requests or sending invitations.

</Step>

<Step title="Open Sub-Account Requests" icon="users">

Go to the organization's team or settings area and open **Sub-Account Requests**.

The pending badge shows how many invitation, join, incoming transfer, and outgoing transfer items still need owner attention.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the matching tab" icon="folder">

Open the tab that matches the item you need to handle:

- **Invites** for pending legacy invitations.
- **Join** for new sub-account requests.
- **Incoming** for transfers into your organization.
- **Outgoing** for transfers away from your organization.

</Step>

<Step title="Take the requested action" icon="check-circle">

Choose **Send**, **Approve**, or **Reject** depending on the request type.

For rejected requests, add a short reason when it will help the requester understand what to change before trying again.

</Step>

<Step title="Review staff capabilities" icon="shield">

After access is active, review the Capability Matrix and confirm the staff capability set matches the person's responsibilities.

If the person needs broader access, toggle only the capabilities they need and save the changes.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

Do not use owner access as a shortcut for routine work. If someone only needs operational access, keep them as staff and adjust staff capabilities instead.

</Callout>

## Transfer a sub-account between organizations

Transfers move a sub-account from one organization to another without requiring the person to start over with a new identity.

A transfer can involve two sides:

- **Source organization**: the organization the sub-account is leaving.
- **Destination organization**: the organization the sub-account is joining.

Owners review transfer requests from **Outgoing** when the sub-account is leaving their organization and **Incoming** when the sub-account is joining their organization. Approved transfers update the sub-account's organization connection and username for the destination organization after the required approvals are complete.

## What staff can see

Staff members can see the Capability Matrix in a read-only view. This helps them understand what their role can and cannot do inside the organization.

Staff do not see the owner request queue unless they have approval-related access. If an action is not available, the most common reason is that the capability is not enabled for staff in that organization.

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Why are approval buttons disabled?">

Approve and reject actions require sub-account approval access. Owners always have that access, while staff only receive it if an owner enables the relevant capability in the Capability Matrix.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why did staff access change for everyone at once?">

The Capability Matrix controls staff capabilities at the organization level. When an owner saves staff capability changes, the update applies to every staff sub-account in that organization.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I delete a request instead of rejecting it?">

Owners reject requests to close them. Rejection can include an optional reason so the requester understands why access was not granted.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Security checklist

Use this checklist when managing organization access.

- Give every team member their own login and sub-account.
- Keep owner access limited to trusted decision-makers.
- Review the Capability Matrix after onboarding or role changes.
- Reject requests you do not recognize.
- Verify source and destination organizations before approving transfers.
- Revisit staff access after sensitive projects, staffing changes, or compliance reviews.
