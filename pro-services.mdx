## Where Pro Services lives

Pro Services appears in the **Post-Production** stage of a Collab project. Use it after the project has moved beyond setup and you are organizing work that supports post-production.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Collab project" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Go to the **Collabs hub** at `/collabs`, then open the project at `/collab/{id}`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Post-Production" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Choose **Post-Production** from the project stage navigation. This stage groups post-production work areas separately from Production and Distribution.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Pro Services" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Select **Pro Services** from the Post-Production child tabs. It appears next to **Project Files** in the current workflow navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it fits into the workflow

Use Pro Services as part of the same project workspace, not as a separate project record. Keeping it inside Post-Production helps you move from planning and collaboration into the post-production work that follows.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Production" icon="play-circle">
    Use Production for the project overview, collaborators, compliance, timeline, moodboard, and screenplay.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Post-Production" icon="edit">
    Use Post-Production for **Project Files** and **Pro Services**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Distribution" icon="upload">
    Use Distribution for **File Sharing**, Metadata, Credits, Licensing, and Forensics & Distribution.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you do not see Pro Services, confirm that you are inside a Collab project and that **Post-Production** is selected. Availability can also depend on the project and account configuration shown in the app.
</Callout>

## Purpose

The Pro Services tab connects your project to additional services, support, or specialized resources during Post-Production. It surfaces options relevant to the current workflow stage so you can coordinate what the project needs without leaving the Collab workspace.

Use it to:

- Identify which post-production services the project requires.
- Assign providers or teams to each service.
- Track service status from scoped through completion.
- Confirm deliverables are accepted before moving to Distribution.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Pro Services is contextual — it appears when the project reaches Post-Production and surfaces options relevant to the current workflow stage.
</Callout>

## When to update it

Update Pro Services whenever the project's post-production needs change.

Typical moments to update:

- **When entering Post-Production**: Review available services once the project reaches this stage.
- **When project scope changes**: New deliverables may require additional services not previously needed.
- **When a service is completed**: Remove or archive completed service engagements to keep the tab current.
- **When new collaborators need support**: Check if Pro Services offers options matching their role.
- **Before distribution**: Confirm all post-production services are finalized before moving to the Distribution stage.

## What to include

- **Service type**
  The category of service (e.g., editing, color grading, sound mixing, VFX) relevant to the project's post-production needs.
- **Service status**
  Whether the service is being scoped, in progress, completed, or on hold.
- **Assigned provider**
  The collaborator, team, or external provider responsible for delivering the service.
- **Dependencies**
  Other services, files, or approvals that must be completed before this service can proceed.
- **Timeline link**
  Connection to the [Schedule](/schedule) tab showing when the service should start and end.
- **Deliverable description**
  What the service produces (e.g., final cut, color-graded master, mixed audio track).

## Best practices

- **Scope services before selecting**: Define what each service must deliver before engaging a provider so expectations are clear.
- **Link services to the Schedule**: Every service engagement should have a corresponding time block in the [Schedule](/schedule).
- **Track dependencies explicitly**: If color grading depends on a locked edit, state that dependency so providers know when they can start.
- **Confirm deliverables before distribution**: No project should move to the Distribution stage with unresolved post-production services.
- **Keep the tab scoped to Post-Production**: Pro Services surfaces options relevant to the current project's workflow stage, not a general marketplace.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Review Pro Services alongside the [Overview](/overview) and [Schedule](/schedule) before each post-production milestone. This helps you catch missing services or unmet dependencies before they block progress.
</Callout>

## Workflow: Coordinate services in Post-Production

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review project needs" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Check the [Overview](/overview) and [Schedule](/schedule) to identify which post-production services the project requires. Match needs to available service options in the Pro Services tab.

    Success looks like: a clear list of required services mapped to available options.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign or request a provider" icon="package" title-type="p">
    For each service, assign a collaborator or team, or request an external provider if the service requires specialized expertise.

    Success looks like: every required service having an assigned provider or a pending request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track service progress" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Monitor service status as work progresses. Update the status when services move from scoped to in progress to completed.

    Success looks like: the Pro Services tab reflecting the real-time status of all post-production engagements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm completion before distribution" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Verify that all required services are completed and deliverables are accepted before moving the project to the Distribution stage.

    Success looks like: zero unresolved post-production services blocking the transition to distribution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Role-specific guidance

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Project owner (sender)" icon="user">
    As the sender, you are responsible for determining which post-production services the project needs and assigning providers.

    - Keep service statuses current so collaborators know what is in progress.
    - Confirm deliverables are accepted before moving to Distribution.
    - Ensure the Pro Services tab stays scoped to actual project requirements.
    - When a service is blocked, identify the dependency and resolve it through the [Schedule](/schedule) or [File Sharing](/file-sharing) tabs.

    Success looks like the Pro Services tab accurately reflecting the state of all post-production work.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Collaborator (receiver)" icon="users">
    As a receiver, check the Pro Services tab to see which services you are assigned and understand dependencies.

    - Review your assigned services and their dependencies before starting work.
    - Update your service status when progress is made.
    - If a service is blocked by an unmet dependency, flag it in project chat.
    - Confirm that your deliverables are accepted before the project moves to Distribution.

    Success looks like you can start and complete your assigned services without unclear expectations or hidden dependencies.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Common issues

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Services listed but never assigned" default-open="false">
    **Cause:** A service type appears in the tab but no provider is assigned, leaving it in limbo.

    **Fix:**

    - Assign a provider or team to the service.
    - Remove the service if it is no longer needed.
    - Check the [Schedule](/schedule) to confirm whether the service still has a time block.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Dependencies not tracked" default-open="false">
    **Cause:** Color grading is listed but the edit isn't locked, and no one flagged the dependency.

    **Fix:**

    - State dependencies explicitly in the service description.
    - Link the dependency to the upstream service or [File Sharing](/file-sharing) entry.
    - Notify the provider when the dependency is resolved so they can start.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Services completed but not marked done" default-open="false">
    **Cause:** The work is finished but the status still shows "in progress," causing confusion about whether the project can move to distribution.

    **Fix:**

    - Update the service status to completed as soon as deliverables are accepted.
    - Verify that the [Schedule](/schedule) reflects the actual completion timeline.
    - Confirm that no downstream services are still waiting on the completed deliverable.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="No link to the Schedule" default-open="false">
    **Cause:** Services are listed without any timing reference, making it impossible to know if post-production is on track.

    **Fix:**

    - Link every service to a time block in the [Schedule](/schedule).
    - Update the Schedule when a service starts earlier or later than planned.
    - Flag timing risks in project chat before they become missed deadlines.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
