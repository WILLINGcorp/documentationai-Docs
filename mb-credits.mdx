## Earn rewards and cover eligible compliance costs

MB Credits is a wallet for tracking rewards earned through activity on ModelBoard. You can use your balance toward eligible project compliance coverage when your organization participates in the project.

Credits have no cash value and never expire. The wallet shows your current balance, available rewards, progress toward coverage, and credit history.

## Find your MB Credits wallet

Open the wallet from either of these locations:

- Open **Settings**, then **Account**.
- Open **Settings**, then **Billing**.
- From the Creator dashboard, select the balance chip. You can also open the [Creator dashboard](/creator-dashboard) to find the wallet shortcut.
- From an organization dashboard, select the balance chip.

The wallet displays the current reward catalog available to your account. Rewards are grouped by activity, and completed rewards show their earned state.

## Earn credits through available rewards

The active catalog can include rewards in these categories:

- **Onboarding:** Complete profile and account setup activities.
- **Profile:** Add or improve profile information.
- **Try a feature:** Use selected ModelBoard features for the first time.
- **Referrals:** Earn credits for eligible verified referrals.

Current seeded examples include the following reward amounts:

- **Onboarding:** Profile photo, 100 credits; display name, 50; legal information, 50; identity verification, 150; finish onboarding, 150.
- **Profile:** Bio or description, 75 credits; banner image, 75; username, 50; location, 50; country, 75; socials or website, 75; skills, 50; content types, 50.
- **Try a feature:** First MB BioLink, 100 credits; first Collab, 100; first casting form, 100.
- **Referrals:** Verified referral, 250 credits per eligible referral.

Reward availability depends on your account type and the active catalog. For example, the casting-form reward is available to studios. The wallet is the source of truth for which rewards apply to you and how many credits each reward provides.

<Callout kind="info">

The reward catalog and amounts can change. Check your MB Credits wallet for the current rewards available to your account.

</Callout>

## Use credits for project compliance coverage

Apply credits from the project's [Compliance](/compliance) tab. You must be a project participant, and your organization must be a party to that project.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the project" icon="folder" title-type="p">

Open a project where your organization participates.

**Success looks like:** The project workspace opens and you can access its compliance information.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the Compliance tab" icon="shield" title-type="p">

Open the project's **Compliance** tab. Review the current compliance coverage cost shown in the product.

**Success looks like:** You can see the coverage option and the number of MB Credits required at the current cost.

</Step>

<Step title="Redeem your credits" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

If your balance meets the displayed cost, select the available coverage action and confirm the redemption.

**Success looks like:** The project shows one compliance-coverage credit applied, and your wallet balance reflects the redemption.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Only eligible project participants can use MB Credits for coverage. The coverage cost is configurable, so the amount shown in the project is the amount that applies to your redemption and may vary over time.

</Callout>

### If your balance is insufficient

The coverage action remains unavailable until your balance meets the current cost. Return to your wallet to review available rewards, complete eligible activities, or check your history before trying again.

## Review your credit history

The wallet history shows how your balance changed over time. Each entry includes:

- The activity or redemption label.
- The date it was recorded.
- The signed amount added to or deducted from your balance.

Use the history to confirm that a reward was credited or that a compliance redemption was applied.
