## What ModelBoard brings together

ModelBoard gives adult-industry creators, studios, businesses, and enterprise operators a shared workspace for public presence, collaboration, compliance, discovery, and integrations. Each feature is shaped by account type, so a creator sees tools for career visibility while a studio sees casting, production, and roster workflows.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

Start with the account type that matches how you work today. ModelBoard can expand from a single creator or business profile into studio workflows, enterprise sub-accounts, and partner integrations as your structure grows.

</Callout>

## How features change by account type

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Creator" icon="users">

Creator features center on career visibility and safer collaborations.

- Build an industry-facing verified profile with portfolio, credits, and attributes.
- Customize a public Link-in-Bio page for external audiences and platform links.
- Send or receive collaboration proposals and follow project details from one workspace.
- Use compliance workflows for releases, consent records, and verification when projects require them.
- Improve discovery through profile completeness, travel plans, portfolio visibility, and network activity.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Business" icon="briefcase">

Business features help service providers, brands, freelancers, and app builders get discovered and manage client work.

- Publish a business directory listing with services, products, categories, reviews, and verification status.
- Promote special offers, industry events, and targeted advertisements to relevant audiences.
- Manage boards for campaigns, client engagements, and service delivery with creators and studios.
- Use or publish app-store integrations when your business provides software, workflow extensions, or niche solutions.
- Coordinate with eligible managed or Enterprise setups when staff access is required for larger operations.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Studio" icon="monitor">

Studio features support casting, production coordination, compliance, and credits across shoots.

- Maintain a verified studio page with productions, roster signals, studio news, and credits.
- Publish casting calls and review structured applications from models and creators.
- Coordinate schedules, call sheets, files, release assets, crew, and production metadata.
- Connect productions to vAulth compliance workflows for releases, consent, and 2257 records.
- Coordinate managed access through eligible Enterprise setups when productions require creator or staff account oversight across multiple connected accounts.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Enterprise" icon="building">

Enterprise features coordinate agencies, holdings, and platforms that operate many accounts or external integrations.

- Provision and manage creator, studio, business, and staff sub-accounts under one umbrella.
- Switch between linked account contexts while preserving permissions and operational boundaries.
- Centralize analytics, compliance posture, and workflow expectations across connected entities.
- Use SDK, OAuth, and App Store integration paths for partner platforms and listed applications.
- Coordinate with ModelBoard on agency, holding, or platform structures when account design affects governance.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Compliance stays attached to the workflow

ModelBoard positions compliance as part of daily collaboration rather than a separate afterthought. vAulth-powered workflows help teams capture verification, releases, consent records, custody, and audit history around the people, projects, and assets they already manage.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">

ModelBoard and vAulth provide compliance tooling, not legal advice. Confirm your record-keeping, consent, and 2257 processes with qualified legal counsel for the jurisdictions where you operate.

</Callout>
