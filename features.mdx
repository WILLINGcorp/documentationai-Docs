## Platform-agnostic 2257 compliance

ModelBoard uses vAulth flows to support 2257 documentation, mobile ID capture, encrypted record storage, and audit-ready status tracking across creator profiles and Collab projects.

Creators can capture ID documents from mobile devices by holding the phone upright and aligning the document with the landscape guide frame. After drawing a signature, they may also authorize vAulth or ModelBoard to save and reuse that signature solely for future automated 2257 document generation.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">

  Always consult legal experts for 2257 compliance. ModelBoard and vAulth provide tools to organize compliance workflows, but they do not provide legal advice.

</Callout>

Use the dashboard and project Compliance tab to upload records, review status, follow vAulth prompts for mobile ID capture, and manage automated signature authorization for eligible 2257 document generation. When delegated signatures are used, review the Vault audit history for the corresponding automated 2257 signature events.
