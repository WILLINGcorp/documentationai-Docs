## Platform-Agnostic 2257 Compliance

Keep compliance records connected to the people and projects that need them. ModelBoard works with vAulth for verification-backed storage, record access, and audit visibility, so teams can review compliance status without moving sensitive documents through production chat or shared folders.

Optional Automated 2257 Signature authorization lets eligible creators consent to ModelBoard-assisted signature handling from their compliance settings. Creators can revoke that authorization when needed, and related consent, revocation, and compliance activity remains visible in the dashboard for review.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Always consult legal experts for 2257 compliance. ModelBoard and vAulth provide workflow and record-management tools, but do not offer legal advice.
</Callout>

Use the dashboard to upload records, link them to profiles and projects, review verification status, manage automated signature authorization, and inspect recent compliance activity.
